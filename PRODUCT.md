# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Small business owners who want to be found, trusted and run better online. Most arrive on a phone from a TikTok or YouTube Short, often mid-task and short on time. Most are not technical. They want to know what to do and want to be able to do it themselves.

## Product Purpose

The AI Business Kit is a blog with free tools. Articles lead. Tools sit inside and beside them at `/tools/<name>`. The goal is audience, not revenue. A visit succeeds when the visitor joins the email list. A visitor who isn't ready to sign up should follow on TikTok or YouTube Shorts, which also counts towards the audience goal.

## Positioning

Plain-English AI guides and free tools that help small businesses get found, trusted and run better online. No jargon, no upsell.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: join the email list.
- Secondary CTA: follow on TikTok or YouTube Shorts.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: plain-English AI help for small businesses, free.
- Belief ladder: not defined yet. Settle it during shape.
- Proof on hand: none yet. Nothing is published. Never invent testimonials, stats or subscriber counts.

## Brand Personality

Clear and capable. A visitor should leave thinking "this explains it plainly, and I could do this myself." The voice is Phil's own: direct, plain, slightly British, measured. It holds an opinion and never oversells (see `docs/writing-style-phil.md`). The visual reference is Runpod's confident, tech-forward look, combined with the HubSpot blog's layout: a featured post, topic categories and a grid of articles that is easy to scan.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI startup: purple gradients, sparkle icons, robot-brain art, glowing blobs.
- Corporate content farm: stock photos of people pointing at laptops, pop-ups, sidebars full of ads.
- Hustle guru: huge promises, countdown timers, "10x your business" headlines, a face on every thumbnail.
- Bare dev blog: a plain monospace list of post titles that gives a non-technical owner no welcome.

## Design Principles

1. Show, don't promise. Use real tools and real examples, never hype.
2. Plain first. A non-technical owner on a phone understands every page.
3. Earn the signup. Value comes before the ask. No pop-ups.
4. Tech-confident, not tech-scary. Runpod's polish, without the jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. This covers contrast, keyboard use, visible focus states and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation.
