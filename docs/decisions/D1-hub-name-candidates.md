# D1: Hub name candidates

Date: 17 Nov 2026 · Decider: Phil · Status: open

## How I checked

- .com: Verisign RDAP (`https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`) on 17 Nov 2026. A 404 means unregistered.
- RDAP shows registry status only. A 404 can still be a name on a premium or aftermarket list, so confirm the price at the registrar before you count on £8-9.
- Handles (X, TikTok, YouTube): not checked for any name. Those sites block cheap automated checks. Check by hand before buying.
- Trademark: not checked. Search the UK IPO register and the USPTO before you commit.

## Top pick

Pavement Board. It gives you a picture you can show in the first second of a Short, and it names no tool, so every future page fits under it. High Street Kit is the safe second choice if you want the name to say "small business" with no explaining.
