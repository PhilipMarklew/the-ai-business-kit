# Handoff: S00-1 Site foundations (Distribution Asset)

Date: 13 Nov 2026 · Owner: Phil · Source of truth: `keyword-shortlist-and-content-plan.xlsx` (Backlog row S00-1, 12-week order W1)

## 1. Why this task, why first

Every other backlog item (13 tools, 12 pillar posts, 27 supporting posts, videos, X posts) publishes onto this site and measures against it. Nothing else can ship until it exists. Budget for this task: **about 6 hours of Phil's time equivalent, week 1 of 12**.

The project goal is audience, not revenue: email subscribers plus social followers. The site is one hub that hosts free tools on isolated pages (`/tools/<name>`), not a tools directory. The theme leads with AI, aimed at small businesses getting found, trusted and run better online.

## 2. Hard constraints (do not break)

- Under £200 total spend for the first 3 months. Prefer free tiers; check current limits before relying on them.
- Phil has 5-7 hrs/week. Anything that creates ongoing admin load is a cost.
- No outreach, no community building, no SaaS, no affiliate or subscription revenue.
- No LinkedIn. Social is TikTok, YouTube Shorts (main) and X (secondary). Video is screen recording plus voiceover, no face.
- Stack: Next.js, React, Tailwind v4, shadcn, pnpm, hosted on Railway. Supabase only if it earns its place. Phil confirmed this stack on 13 Nov 2026 after comparing Astro (a content-first alternative); do not reopen it.
- Tools will later live at `/tools/<name>` as isolated pages. The URL pattern and layout must allow that without rework.
- Copy goes through the `writing-style-phil` skill and `stop-slop`. Direct, plain, slightly British, no even-paragraph rhythm.
